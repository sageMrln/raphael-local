Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

Raphael Local is a personal desktop app. Only its owner signs in with it, using his own Google accounts.

What it reads

With the owner's consent it reads his Google Drive files and his Gmail messages. It may label, archive or delete his own mail when he asks it to.

Where the data goes

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept encrypted on that computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Raphael Local's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
